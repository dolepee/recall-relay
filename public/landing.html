Workflow

Three steps. One record.

The notice sets the requirement. The customer supplies evidence. You make the approval.

01 / PREPARE

Prepare claim

Confirm the affected item and tell the customer exactly what to send.

02 / REVIEW

Review evidence

Compare the evidence with the notice. Request a correction only when something is missing.

03 / REFUND

Approve refund

Approve the affected item only and close the claim with a provider-confirmed record.

The record

From notice to payment, one trail.

No side spreadsheet. The source, the evidence revision, the explicit merchant approval and the refund confirmation stay attached to the claim.

01

Source stays quoted

CPSC 27-026 is imported read-only. Each requirement keeps its citation.

02

Evidence stays versioned

A correction creates a new revision, clears the prior review and asks for fresh evidence.

03

Approval has a name

An explicit merchant approval is recorded against the revision it was made on. A repeat approval resumes that one request and re-checks the capture first.

04

Payment has a receipt

The receipt is written from PayPal's confirmation of the refund, never from the request you sent.

Trust

Built for the part that has to be right.

The notice is the source.

Public source text, citations and the original remedy stay visible. The desk does not rewrite the requirement to fit the claim.

The evidence is a version.

Corrections are a review detail, not a new workflow. The previous submission stays attached; a change reopens the review.

The money is bounded.

Affected item only, back to the original payment. Sandbox workspace, test funds, fictional merchandise; practice refunds are simulated.

Questions

Short answers.

Is this connected to PayPal?

The sandbox desk uses PayPal Sandbox test funds. The practice claim never contacts a payment provider, and its refund is simulated only.

Can I refund part of an order?

Only the affected item is in scope. The original order stays linked, and the unaffected line is excluded from the refund.

What happens after a correction?

The claim keeps the previous evidence and creates a new revision. The merchant must review that revision again before any refund.
