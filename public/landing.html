02How it works

Five handoffs. One claim.

The notice is public. The evidence is the customer's. The approval is yours. Each step leaves a record the next person can read.

  1. 01 / Source

    The notice comes in read-only

    A public CPSC notice is imported with its remedy, product and source reference. The words stay quoted, not rewritten.

  2. 02 / Requirement

    The remedy becomes checks

    Each requirement cites the sentence it came from. You can add one by hand, and the revision goes back for review.

  3. 03 / Customer correction

    A missing face comes back

    The first photograph misses a marked side. You ask for a correction; the customer resubmits on the same private link.

  4. 04 / Merchant approval

    A person signs off the amount

    You approve the affected line only — $25.99, to the original payment — and the approval is tied to this revision.

  5. 05 / Receipt

    PayPal confirms, then it is done

    The receipt is written from PayPal's confirmation of the refund, never from the request you sent.

04Trust

Three things it will not do.

Preparing evidence is judgement. Moving money is arithmetic and discipline. The product is built to keep those apart.

Guard 01

Pay without a person

Every refund needs an explicit merchant approval, recorded against the revision it was made on. Change the evidence and the approval no longer stands.

There is no autonomous payment mode to switch on.

Guard 02

Pay the same claim twice

A claim takes an exclusive reservation before anything is dispatched. A second approval finds it reserved and stops.

An unconfirmed dispatch is held and reconciled against PayPal, never blindly retried.

Guard 03

Call it resolved early

No receipt is written until PayPal reads the refund back. No readback, no receipt, and the claim stays open.

Amounts are held as exact integer cents.

No AI authority

The model may draft wording for you to review. It cannot approve a claim, dispatch a refund or write a receipt.

05Scope

What this is, precisely.

A sandbox evaluation service for one narrow job: resolve a claim that has already arrived.

+You bring

  • —A PayPal Sandbox buyer account to approve the test purchase.
  • —An order whose original capture you still hold, in USD.
  • —One photograph per claim, plus the confirmations the notice asks for.
  • —A customer you can send the private claim link to.

−It does not

  • —Find or notify affected customers. You share the link with someone you already know bought the item.
  • —Import your order book; matching is supplied by the sandbox fixture.
  • —Contact an agency or manufacturer, or enrol you in any programme.
  • —Move real money, or refund without an explicit merchant approval.
